About the club

Meet the squad.

We're a bunch of K-State students who believe the best study break is a board in the middle of the table and someone about to flip it.

What we do

Roll & Rally is a board games club for university students. We host regular game nights where you can learn something new, run it back with an old favorite, or just vibe with snacks while others play.

  • Open tables: grab a seat, no experience needed.
  • Game rotation: strategy classics, party chaos, and nostalgia picks.
  • Teach-it-to-me nights: someone always knows the rules (and will explain them twice).
  • Mini tournaments: bragging rights only, but the bragging is elite.

Our mission

To give every student a screen-free spot on campus to connect, compete, and laugh, where everyone belongs at the table, whether you're a grandmaster or still figuring out how the dice work.

How the club started (a true-ish tale)

It all began during a very serious group project meeting at Hale Library. Somebody pulled out a game "just for five minutes" to unwind. Forty-five minutes later, the project was untouched, a chess match had turned into a full-blown rivalry, and a librarian had politely asked us to "keep the dramatic gasps down."

Then came the Betrayal incident. One friend was revealed as the traitor, took it extremely personally, and didn't speak to the group until the next afternoon. Everyone agreed that this was the best night of the semester.

We figured campus needed more of that energy: low-stakes drama, high-stakes snacks, and zero notifications. So we made it official. And yes, the group project did eventually get finished, with a very sleepy A-minus.
