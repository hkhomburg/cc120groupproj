The Founding Clowns

Three original creators, three very different game faces.

Hannah Homburg

★ Grand Ringmaster

Clown Hierarchy: Tier 1 of 5

Gaming background

Grew up with a game closet that was basically a library. Got hooked on strategy games early and has been running the table at family game nights ever since. Brings the organized energy, and the snacks.

Top board game picks

  1. Chess
  2. Catan
  3. Codenames

Why this rank?

Keeps the whole show running on time while somehow looking unbothered. Wears the top hat. Controls the whistle.

Ian Godines

★ Chief Juggler

Clown Hierarchy: Tier 2 of 5

Gaming background

A lifelong fan of games with a twist, especially the ones where friendships get tested. Started with the classics, leveled up to full-on spooky campaigns, and now can't resist a good plot twist.

Top board game picks

  1. Betrayal at House on the Hill
  2. Clue
  3. Ticket to Ride

Can keep ten rules, six players, and one suspicious traitor in the air at the same time. Has never dropped the dice (that we know of).

Maddox Dorland
