This Week's Lineup

Four games, infinite chaos. Pick your fighter.

♟️

Chess

  • 2 players
  • 30+ min
  • Big brain

The ultimate main character move. Plot your plan, flex your galaxy brain, and watch your opponent spiral when you pull off that fork. It's giving grandmaster. Beginners welcome, no cap.

🏚️

Betrayal at House on the Hill

  • 3–6 players
  • 60 min
  • Plot twist

Explore a haunted mansion with your besties until someone turns on the group. Low-key iconic, extremely unhinged. Trust issues? You'll have them by the end. Main quest: don't get ghosted (literally).

🍭

Candy Land

  • 2–4 players
  • 15 min
  • Zero stress

Pure nostalgia, straight from your childhood. No strategy, just colors, sugar, and vibes. It's the ultimate midterm-week reset. Slay the Gumdrop Pass and feel like a kid again. Immaculate.

🪜

Chutes and Ladders

  • 2–4 players
  • 20 min
  • Pure luck

Climb up, slide down, repeat. Your rizz means nothing here because the dice run the show. It's the most dramatic comeback (or fall off) you'll see all week. Bestie, we're so back. Wait, we're so not.
